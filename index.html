ABOUT YUCCA

From Western Cape Origins to Global Partner

Founded in Paarl in 2002, Yucca Packaging has grown into one of Southern Africa’s premier packaging innovators. With our state-of-the-art logistics hub in Brackenfell, Western Cape, we partner with leading agricultural growers, food franchise chains, and international exporters.

Through strategic global alliances—such as our pioneering partnership with Multisteps—we introduce cutting-edge thermoforming technologies, top-seal automation, and light-weighted designs to the African continent.
